Experimental singing timing pipeline / sung-reading-v1

Inference weights download separately on first use. No song audio, lyric text or
model weights are included in the distributed app.

Demucs: Alexandre Defossez and collaborators. Demucs MLX conversion/runtime by
ssmall256. https://huggingface.co/ssmall256/demucs-mlx
Runtime and model card label: MIT. Original model-weight terms are disputed in
https://github.com/facebookresearch/demucs/issues/327; retain that distinction.
Review the original weight terms before using this optional experimental model.

Kim Vocal 2: Mel-Band-RoFormer weights by Kim, MLX conversion by Prince Canuma
and contributors. https://huggingface.co/mlx-community/mel-roformer-kim-vocal-2-mlx
MIT, copyright (c) 2024 Kim and contributors; copyright (c) 2026 Prince Canuma.
The full checkpoint LICENSE is downloaded and verified beside its weights.
Inference module is from mlx-audio 0.4.3, MIT; wheel notices are bundled under
ExperimentalPython. Only the MelRoFormer module is loaded, not its other models.

MMS karaoke Japanese Latn: NextFire.
https://huggingface.co/NextFire/mms-300m-ForcedAligner-karaoke-ja-Latn
Model card: CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/
Derived from facebook/mms-300m, Meta Platforms, Inc.:
https://huggingface.co/facebook/mms-300m
Base weights: CC BY-NC 4.0 https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/
Transformers inference code: Apache 2.0, notices bundled under ExperimentalPython.

TIFA and its constituent dictionaries/converters: see TIFA notices in this bundle.
Exact downloaded bytes, hashes, revisions and sizes: refinement-model-files.json
inside the experimental runtime. Tests measure retained coverage, not listening
accuracy. Independent evidence gates repairs; prior results remain selectable.

Singing phoneme recognizer: Xiantaidu/RomajiASR release v1.0.0.
https://github.com/Xiantaidu/RomajiASR
Source license retained from commit 79f92eedc7e8f87ca3bc4dcf5d3178cb68c550af.
Source and released singing HuBERT weights: Apache 2.0. Derived from
prj-beatrice/japanese-hubert-base and rinna/japanese-hubert-base (Apache 2.0).
Released training config uses ambiguity CTC; inference uses the equivalent stock
Transformers HuBERT implementation, checked against the released ONNX model.
